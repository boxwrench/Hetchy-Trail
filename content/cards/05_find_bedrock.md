## Description

Test borings reveal that the foundation excavation will be much deeper than the visible valley floor suggests. Full excavation and river diversion must be funded before concrete placement can continue.

## Historical fact

Work required a roughly 1,000-foot diversion tunnel and deep excavation through glacial boulders, sand and other material before sound bedrock could be reached. The initial dam was completed in 1923 after continuous, all-season work.

## Source note

SFPUC 2005 history, "Dam & Tunnel Construction Starts in the High Sierra", p. 34 [SFPUC-2005]. Design mapping: docs/Hetchy Trail Historical Game Mapping.pdf, section 5.

## Assumption note

There was no cheap alternative to full excavation, so this card offers a single choice. Requiring construction power first mirrors Early Intake electricity reaching the dam site before major dam construction began in 1919.
