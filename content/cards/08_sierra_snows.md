## Description

Snow closes the mountain roads. Without the railroad, cement, food and equipment cannot reach the high camps until the passes clear.

## Historical fact

Mountain roads to the project were regularly blocked by snow. The Hetch Hetchy Railroad, completed in 1917, operated through winter and kept supplies moving when the roads could not.

## Source note

SFPUC 2005 history, "Early Intake Powerhouse and Lake Eleanor", p. 32 [SFPUC-2005]. Records that motor roads were snowbound. Also NPS [NPS-YOSE] on year-round rail movement. Design mapping: docs/Hetchy Trail Historical Game Mapping.pdf, section 5.

## Assumption note

This card is gameplay-derived rather than a specific documented incident: it exists so the railroad decision has a visible consequence. The underlying condition (snowbound roads, railroad as the winter lifeline) is historical.
