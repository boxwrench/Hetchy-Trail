## Description

Complete the cableway and lower the siphon sections before access to the canyon becomes more difficult.

## Historical fact

The aqueduct crossed Red Mountain Bar through a 770-foot steel inverted siphon anchored in bedrock and concrete. The crossing was necessary because the canyon would be affected by the filling of Don Pedro Reservoir. A long cableway transported workers and loads across the canyon.

## Source note

SFPUC 2005 history, "Foothill Tunnel", p. 37 [SFPUC-2005]. Design mapping: docs/Hetchy Trail Historical Game Mapping.pdf, section 5.
