## Description

Wait for favorable conditions, install deep foundations and include pressure relief equipment rather than treating the valley as a simple surface crossing.

## Historical fact

Portions of the pipelines descended below sea level and passed beneath the San Joaquin River and Elliott Cut. Pipes were supported on timber piles and protected with concrete jackets. Relief valves were included to control excess pressure.

## Source note
