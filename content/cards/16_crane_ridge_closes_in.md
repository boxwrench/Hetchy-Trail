## Description

Previously reliable timber supports begin crushing. Pause excavation and adopt the larger gunite-ring design, or press on with the old method.

## Historical fact

At Crane Ridge, approximately 2,500 feet below the surface, swelling ground squeezed the excavated opening. In one reported section, an approximately 18-foot opening contracted to about three feet within 24 hours. Crews responded by excavating an oversized bore and constructing gunite support rings with room for the ground to move.

## Source note

SFPUC 2005 history, "Coast Range Tunnel", p. 39 [SFPUC-2005]. Design mapping: docs/Hetchy Trail Historical Game Mapping.pdf, section 5.

## Assumption note

Both choices grant the same readiness because the section was ultimately completed either way; the difference is time and crew cost.
