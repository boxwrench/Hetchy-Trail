## Description

Install the siphon sections and coordinate the crossing with completion of the Coast Range headings.

## Historical fact

The Coast Range system connected to Irvington through a multiple-pipe inverted siphon across Alameda Creek and the Sunol area. Alameda Creek Siphon No. 1 was completed in February 1934.

## Source note

SFPUC 2005 history, "San Joaquin Pipelines", p. 38 [SFPUC-2005]. Describes the multiple-pipe inverted siphon across Alameda Creek. One detail is not in the cited pages; see docs/SOURCES.md, open items. Design mapping: docs/Hetchy Trail Historical Game Mapping.pdf, section 5.
